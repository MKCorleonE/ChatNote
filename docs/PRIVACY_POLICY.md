## 简体中文

生效日期：2026年10月13日 · ChatNote 0.12.0

ChatNote是由独立开发者维护、以本机保存为主的学习笔记应用。本政策说明当前桌面版、本次选择的模型服务和自愿联系渠道如何处理数据。查看本页不会发送数据，也不会替你开通服务。

### 1. 本机保存的数据

对话、回答、精确原文锚点、追问、个人笔记、草稿、附件、连接元数据、偏好和回收站时间保存在你的设备上。桌面版使用本机SQLite数据库，浏览器预览使用浏览器存储。当前没有开发者托管的对话同步、广告或内置统计上传。ChatNote不会加密本机数据库和导出文件，请妥善保护设备和备份。

### 2. 密钥与账号访问

API密钥、ChatGPT登录令牌及客户端注册密文由系统凭据库保管，工作区备份和导出不包含这些秘密信息。连接名称、地址、模型ID属于元数据。ChatGPT登录会与其认证服务通信；退出账号或移除密钥与删除对话是独立操作。

### 3. 什么内容会离开设备

发送问题时，所选服务商会收到本次问题、模型指令、选定上下文和支持的附件。主线默认不包含追问分支；追问使用来源快照及本条追问历史。可主动预览并修改记住的选择。个人笔记和未发送草稿不会自动加入聊天请求。使用模型整理笔记会发送单独预览的材料。录音在请求转写前留在本机，转写时才发送给所选语音服务商。本机预览和普通导出不需要模型推理。

### 4. 服务商与网络服务

登录、模型目录检查、余额查询和模型请求会连接你配置的服务。连接及额度检查不会自动生成回答。这些服务会收到IP地址、认证信息等正常网络数据，其服务地域、保留时间、训练用途、收费和删除规则由自身政策及你的账号条款决定。请仅使用可信地址。ChatNote无法保证删除已经发送给服务商的数据；文档临时文件清理失败时会尽可能提示。

### 5. 保留与删除

删除对话会把完整对话、追问和附件移入回收站，保留24小时；过期内容在下次启动时清理，不会在阅读过程中按截止时间后台擦除。清理前可恢复，也可立即永久删除选中内容或清空回收站。永久清理会移除对应的应用恢复记录及不再被其他记录引用的附件。单条回答删除仍是独立操作。这是应用层删除，不承诺磁盘级安全擦除；外部备份、导出文件及服务商副本不会自动删除。

### 6. 备份、导出与自愿反馈

完整工作区备份包含正常对话、回收站对话及其引用附件，但不包含凭据。笔记和思维导图包含你导出的对话材料，未决定分享前请保持私密。联系页只展示邮箱及小红书号，复制不会发送消息。主动联系开发者时，你的邮箱或账号名称以及自愿发送的内容可能为回应而被阅读、保留；邮箱和小红书平台按各自政策处理通信。请勿发送密钥、登录令牌或不必要的私人对话。

### 7. 你的选择与政策更新

你可以查看上下文、更换服务商、移除密钥、退出登录、备份或导出数据、恢复回收站对话以及本机删除。阅读和本机笔记管理无需模型连接。需要更正或删除你主动发送给开发者的反馈时，可通过下列方式联系；服务商持有的数据需向对应服务商处理。重要变化会在应用政策和更新日志中说明，并更新生效日期。

## English

Effective: 13 October 2026 · ChatNote 0.12.0

ChatNote is a local-first learning notebook maintained by an independent developer. This policy describes the data handled by the current desktop app, your model providers, and voluntary contact channels. Reading this page does not send data or enroll you in a service.

### 1. Data on your device

Conversations, answers, exact passage anchors, follow-ups, personal notes, drafts, attachments, connection metadata, preferences, and recycle bin timestamps are stored on your device. The desktop app uses a local SQLite database; browser previews use browser storage. ChatNote currently has no developer-hosted conversation sync, advertising, or built-in analytics upload. Local databases and exported files are not encrypted by ChatNote; protect your device and backups.

### 2. Keys and account access

API keys, ChatGPT sign-in tokens, and client registration secrets are held in the system credential vault. Workspace backups and exports exclude these secrets. Connection names, addresses, and model IDs are metadata. ChatGPT sign-in communicates with its authentication service; disconnecting an account or removing a key is separate from deleting a conversation.

### 3. What leaves your device

When you send a question, the selected provider receives the current question, model instructions, the chosen context, and supported attachments. Main conversations exclude follow-up branches by default; a follow-up uses its source snapshot and its own history. You can preview and change the remembered selection. Personal notes and unsent drafts are not automatically added to chat requests. Model-assisted note organization sends its separately previewed materials. Recording stays local until you request transcription; then audio is sent to the selected speech provider. Local previews and ordinary exports do not require inference.

### 4. Provider and network services

Sign-in, model directory checks, balance checks, and model requests communicate with the configured service. Connection and quota checks do not automatically generate an answer. Those services receive normal network information such as IP addresses and authentication data. Their location, retention, model training, charges, and deletion rules follow their own policies and your account terms. Use only endpoints you trust. ChatNote does not guarantee removal of data already sent to a provider; document cleanup failures are reported when available.

### 5. Retention and deletion

Deleting a conversation moves the whole conversation, its follow-ups and attachments to the recycle bin for 24 hours. Expired items are cleaned when the app next opens; no background deadline timer erases them while you read. You can restore before cleanup, permanently delete selected items, or empty the bin. Permanent cleanup removes the corresponding app recovery records and attachments that no remaining record uses. Deleting individual answers remains a separate operation. This is application-level deletion, not a guarantee of forensic disk erasure. External backups, exported files, and provider copies are not automatically removed.

### 6. Backups, exports, and voluntary feedback

A full workspace backup includes active and recycled conversations and their referenced attachments, but excludes credentials. Notes and mind maps include the conversation material you export. Keep these files private unless you choose to share them. The contact page displays an email address and Xiaohongshu ID; copying them does not send a message. If you contact the developer, your address or account name and the content you voluntarily send may be read and retained to respond. Email and Xiaohongshu handle that communication under their own policies. Never send keys, sign-in tokens, or unnecessary private conversations.

### 7. Your choices and policy changes

You can inspect context, change providers, remove keys, disconnect sign-in, back up or export data, restore recycled conversations, and delete them locally. Reading and local note management work without a model connection. To discuss correction or deletion of feedback you sent to the developer, use the contacts below; provider-held data must be addressed with that provider. Material changes will be reflected in the app policy and release notes with a new effective date.
